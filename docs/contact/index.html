Our most active chat — we're closing in on 700 members. Similar to Discord but with much better Terms of Service, mostly open-source, and has features similar to Discord Nitro but free.

Telegram

Our official Telegram group. Fast, feature-rich, and cross-platform. Perfect for quick conversations and staying updated with our community.

Open: t.me/+zNkDGCUw65U5Mjcx

Fluxer

A newer open-source chat platform built in Sweden. Features voice/video with screen sharing, noise suppression, moderation tools, custom emojis, and self-hosting option. No email required to join.

Open: fluxer.gg/3cDaqBp6

GameVox

Crystal clear voice, spatial audio for gaming (GameLink), soundboard, screen share, video chat, and forums. Our server is self-hosted by our old friends at Mega Utilities — they host it and we appreciate it! Find them here for links to all their other chats and socials.

Open: gamevox.com/GCAG-5935

Discord

No longer accepting invites.

We no longer support Discord due to their abuse of users' good faith in centralized chat. We left it open as a courtesy to our old members — it was the first chat server we made, RIP.
