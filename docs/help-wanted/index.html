Help Wanted — Volunteer Opportunities

ZamRock Radio is a community-powered station. Here's how you can help us grow and keep the psychedelic sounds flowing!

📡

Host a Relay

Run a Liquidsoap/Icecast relay to mirror our stream. Use a VPS, a home PI, or any always-on Linux box. Every relay takes load off our main server and makes playback snappier for listeners in your region.

  • 🎧 Any host works — VPS, Raspberry PI, or spare Linux box
  • ⚡ Lightweight — 128k, 192k, or 320k bitrate options
  • 📡 Reduces lag and buffering for nearby listeners
  • 🏆 We'll list your relay on our "Network" page
  • 📱 We'll shout you out on Mastodon when you go live
Offer a Relay
📰

Flyer Campaigns

Be our street-level ambassador! We'll send you print-ready PDFs/PNGs — you decide where they go: record stores, cafes, music venues, bulletin boards, or your own front fence.

  • 🖨️ We design and ship the files — you pick the spots
  • 📍 Stick them up in your neighborhood, campus, or scene hubs
  • 📸 Snap a photo of your flyer in the wild — we repost!
  • 🎁 Every postcard-worthy spot becomes a story we share
  • 📡 Want a custom QR code? Just ask when you contact us
Get Flyer Files
🏷️

Sticker Resellers

Got a shop? Become an official ZamRock sticker reseller. We'll feature your store on our website's "Where to Buy" section and drop a thank-you post on our social feeds.

  • 🏪 Perfect for record stores, cafes, music shops, and zine distros
  • 📦 We ship the starter pack upfront — no upfront cost
  • 💰 You keep a cut of every sale, we handle the tracking
  • 🤝 "Official ZamRock Reseller" digital badge for your site
  • 📱 Boosted on our Mastodon and social stories
Apply to Resell
📱

Social Media

Help us grow our Mastodon and social presence. If you live and breathe social media, we want you to help craft posts, create visuals, and engage with the psychedelic music community.

  • ✍️ Draft posts about new music drops and themed weeks
  • 📸 Design shareable graphics, memes, and story templates
  • 📡 Grow our follower count by engaging the scene
  • 🎵 Curate weekly "Now Spinning" and "Throwback" posts
  • 📱 We'll credit you in posts and pin your profile link
Join Media Team
🗄️

Library Mirrors

Our music library is always hungry for storage. Host a mirror on your VPS, dedicated server, or even a high-capacity Raspberry PI with an external drive. We provide the rsync scripts — you provide the space.

  • 💾 We give you the sync scripts (rsync over SSH)
  • 🔒 Secure, read-only access — safe for your box
  • 📡 100Mbps+ preferred, but every bit helps
  • 🤝 Your name/link on our "Thanks" page forever
  • 🏆 We'll write a "New Mirror" shout-out post for you
Offer Storage
✍️

Music Blog Writers

Love writing about psychedelic rock, Afrobeat, funk, and rare grooves? Write for our blog and help listeners discover hidden gems from our daily playlists. Your words, your voice, our platform.

  • 🎵 Review albums and tracks from our daily rotations
  • 🎤 Interview guest DJs and emerging artists
  • 📰 Monthly "What's New" and "Deep Cuts" roundups
  • 🔗 We backlink to your personal blog or portfolio
  • 📱 We'll excerpt your posts on our social feeds
Write for Us
🎧

Guest DJs

Got a killer mix that fits our psychedelic/funk/afrobeat vibe? Send it in and we'll slot you on the schedule, promote your set on our site, and drop a "Guest DJ" badge for your socials.

  • 🎶 Send us your mix (MP3/FLAC, 60-90 min, clean transitions)
  • 📡 We'll feature you on our public schedule for a week
  • 🎵 Showcase your own tracks or curated deep-cuts vibes
  • 🏆 "Guest DJ" badge + featured post on our social media
  • 🔗 Your mix stays in our archives with full credit
Apply to DJ

Dev Help

We've got open-source repos (bSky-Bot, Matrix-Bridge, Stoat, Lang) and closed-source ones that go beta when ready. Scan our hub, pick a project, and contribute code, docs, or testing.

  • 💻 We've got 15+ active projects in various stages of development
  • 📖 bSky-Bot, Matrix-Bridge, Stoat, Lang — all open-source
  • 🔒 Closed-source tools (Mega-Bridger, Vector-Apps) go beta when solid
  • 🎵 Code contributions, docs, testing, or bug reports all welcome
  • 📱 We'll feature you on our "Contributors" page + social shoutout
