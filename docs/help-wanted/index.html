🚨 Help Wanted — Volunteer Opportunities

ZamRock Radio is a community-powered station. Here's how you can help us grow and keep the psychedelic sounds flowing!

📡

Host a Relay

Run a Liquidsoap/Icecast relay to mirror our stream. Helps spread the load and makes playback more reliable for listeners near you.

  • 🎧 Helps spread out load on resources
  • ⚡ Means more reliable playback for your region
  • 🎚 Can be low-bitrate (128k) or high-bitrate (320k)
  • 🏆 Bragging rights as a station supporter
  • 🔗 We'll link back to your site/project
Contact Us to Setup
📰

Flyer Campaigns

Help us get the word out! We need people to distribute flyers in their city, record stores, cafes, and music venues.

  • 🖨️ We provide the flyer designs (PDF/PNG)
  • 📍 You choose the locations in your area
  • 📸 Send us photos of your flyers in the wild!
  • 🎁 We'll shout you out on our social media
Get Flyer Files

Love our stickers? Become an official reseller! We're looking for third-party merch outlets to carry ZamRock Radio stickers.

  • 🏪 Perfect for record stores, cafes, music shops

Library Mirrors

Our music library needs friendly SFTP hosts. Storage is hard to get — we're always looking for reliable mirrors to host our collection.

  • 💾 We provide the sync scripts (rsync-based)
  • 🔒 Secure SFTP-only access for safety
  • 📡 High bandwidth preferred (100Mbps+)
  • 🤝 We'll credit you on our "Thanks" page
